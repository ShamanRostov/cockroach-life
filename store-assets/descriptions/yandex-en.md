# Yandex Games — Cockroach Life

## Title
**Cockroach Life**

## Short description (≤80 chars)
Cockroach sim: junk nest building, raids, arcade survival & colony breeding

## Full description

Welcome to Apartment 47 — except you're not the human with the slipper. You're the cockroach with ambitions. **Cockroach Life** is a browser survival sim from floor level: build an empire from cardboard boxes and old socks, breed an army of antennaed heroes, and raid rival colonies before the humans reach for the bug spray.

### Build your nest from household junk
An isometric grid becomes your fortress. Kitchen in a crumb-filled box, bedroom inside a sock, storage in a tin can, nursery in an egg carton, hospital from a bandage — each room earns passive income and unlocks new options. Upgrade buildings to level 5, set traps (slipper, spray, glue), and unlock a second district — the **balcony** with planters and railing shelters.

### Survive the arcade minigames
When resources run low — take risks:
- **Dodge the Slipper** — reflexes and leaderboard records
- **Bug Spray** — sprint to wall cracks for cover
- **Food Hunt** — collect crumbs before you starve
- **Cat Chase** — a new kitchen threat
- **Hospital** — timing minigame to heal up

Keyboard on PC, virtual joystick on mobile.

### World map and raids
Open the apartment map and scout 11 districts — from the Stove Clan to the Seed Syndicate on the balcony. Every raid has three phases: infiltration, looting the enemy nest, and escaping the slipper. Energy is limited; shields block counter-raids. Climb the **raid rating** and share victories with friends.

### Breeding and roles
Build a Nursery and hatch three roles: **Worker** (income), **Scout** (speed), **Fighter** (raid power). Roaches named Skitter and Crumb have personality. Grow the colony — grow the bonuses.

### Weekly events and daily rewards
A new live event every week: Slipper Week, Spray Week, Raid Week, Build Week, or Food Frenzy. Log in daily for streak bonuses and quests — build rooms, play arcades, complete raids.

### Leaderboards
Compete in three boards: Slipper high score, raid rating, and colony size. Each board updates after your run — beat CrumbKing and TrashHero from rival districts!

### Economy and progression
Three resources keep the colony alive: **food** feeds breeding and construction, **money** buys rooms and upgrades, **health** recovers in the hospital after failed arcades. Passive income grows with every upgraded building — at high levels you can play idle and return for rewards. The Nursery unlocks breeding, Storage expands capacity, Hospital cuts downtime after defeats.

### Nest defense
You're not only the attacker — rivals raid you too. In build mode, place **traps**: slipper, spray can, or glue trap. Rival bots respect your defenses. Buy a **24-hour shield** in the shop to farm resources safely.

### Shop and monetization
The in-game shop offers food and money packs, raid energy refills, and ad removal. Optional rewarded ads double raid loot, grant instant builds, or extra energy — no hard paywalls on core gameplay.

### Tutorial and accessibility
A step-by-step tutorial walks you through your first Kitchen, Food Hunt arcade, and world map. Controls work on PC and phone: WASD, arrows, or virtual joystick. Mute with one tap. Progress auto-saves in the browser.

### Why Cockroach Life?
A rare sim from an **insect's POV**: don't build a city for humans — build an empire under the washing machine. Gentle humor, warm art, sessions as short or long as you like. Perfect for a break at work, on transit, or on the couch.
